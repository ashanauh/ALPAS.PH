Common STDs

  • Chlamydia

    A bacterial infection that can affect the genitals, rectum, and throat. Often has no symptoms but can cause pain and discharge.

  • Gonorrhea

    Another bacterial infection affecting the genitals, rectum, and throat. Can cause burning during urination and unusual discharge.

  • Syphilis

    A bacterial infection that progresses in stages, starting with sores, then rashes, and potentially affecting internal organs if untreated.

  • Human Papillomavirus (HPV)

    A viral infection with many types; some can cause genital warts, others can lead to cancer.

  • Herpes (HSV-1 & HSV-2)

    A viral infection causing painful blisters or sores around the mouth or genitals.

  • HIV/AIDS

    A virus that attacks the immune system, making it harder to fight infections. Without treatment, it can progress to AIDS.

  • Trichomoniasis

    A parasitic infection causing itching, burning, and unusual discharge, mostly in women.

  • Hepatitis B

    A viral infection affecting the liver, which can be transmitted sexually, through blood, or from mother to child.

How to Prevent STDs

Use Condoms

Use Condoms

Condoms protect against STDs during sexual activity. Always use consistently and correctly.

Limit Sexual Partners

Limit Sexual Partners

Fewer partners = lower risk of contracting STDs. Mutual monogamy with a tested partner is safest.

Get Vaccinated

Get Vaccinated

Vaccines like HPV and Hepatitis B help prevent certain STDs. Ask your doctor which ones you need.

Regular Testing

Regular Testing

Early detection makes treatment easier and prevents spread. Get tested regularly.

Communicate with Partner

Communicate with Partner

Honest discussion about sexual health, testing history, and protection ensures safer choices for both.

St. Luke’s Medical Center (STI Unit)

Available in Global City (Taguig) and Quezon City.

Provides specialized care for sexually transmitted infections.

Manila Social Hygiene Clinic

Public sexual health services and treatment hub.

Makati Medical Center

Tropical & Travel Medicine services for infection treatment.

Medi Linx Laboratory (Quezon City)

Offers STD LabCheQ diagnostic packages for screening.

Key Services Offered

  • Bacterial Infections: Targeted treatment with antibiotics for syphilis, gonorrhea, and chlamydia.
  • Viral Infections: Management for HIV and Herpes (HSV).
  • Skin Conditions: Treatment for genital warts and molluscum using creams, freezing, or laser therapy.
  • Vaccinations: HPV (Gardasil 9) and Hepatitis vaccines.
